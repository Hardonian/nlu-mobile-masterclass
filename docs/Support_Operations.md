# Support Operations

## Support levels

- Level 1: learner navigation, access, and course-content questions.
- Level 2: simulator, PWA, or progress-state defects.
- Level 3: reference-policy, security, privacy, or deployment incidents.

## Required ticket context

Capture product version, build ID, browser/device, lesson or scenario ID, reproduction steps, and whether sensitive data was involved. Do not request customer account data in learner support tickets.

## Escalation

Security/privacy concerns bypass normal queueing. Use the incident runbook and preserve only approved opaque identifiers.
