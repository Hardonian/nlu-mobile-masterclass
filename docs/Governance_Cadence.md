# Governance Cadence

## Weekly during pilot

- Review learner completion, assessment misses, accessibility defects, and content questions.
- Triage safety or privacy reports immediately.
- Assign every action an owner and due date.

## Monthly after launch

- Review product metrics by course version, role, locale, and channel.
- Audit vendor-specific claims and external links.
- Review threat-model changes, dependency updates, and rollback readiness.

## Quarterly

- Re-run the accessibility matrix and incident/rollback drill.
- Review commercial packaging and support load.
- Retire or revise stale scenarios and platform guidance.
