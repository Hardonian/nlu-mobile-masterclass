# Role-Based Learning Tracks

## Product and conversation design

Focus: bounded journeys, state maps, repairs, handoff, success metrics, and capstone review.

## Engineering

Focus: normalized contracts, adapters, backend authority, idempotency, events, testing, and deployment.

## Support and operations

Focus: escalation reasons, context transfer, incident response, service expectations, and quality review.

## Security, privacy, and risk

Focus: data minimization, threat modeling, retrieval integrity, access control, retention, and release gates.

All tracks share the same safety principles and capstone release gates.
