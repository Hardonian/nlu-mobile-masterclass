# Product Requirements — Conversational NLU for Mobile

## Product definition

An offline-friendly, mobile-first masterclass that teaches teams to design, evaluate, and safely productize conversational experiences across Dialogflow CX and Amazon Lex V2.

## Primary users

- Conversation designers and product managers who need a safe design framework.
- Engineers who need a provider-neutral orchestration model.
- Facilitators who need a repeatable workshop and assessment kit.

## MVP promise

Within one guided session, a learner can define one bounded journey, distinguish intent from authority, choose a safe route, design recovery and handoff, and produce a review-ready release brief.

## Success metrics

- 70% of started learners complete the eight lessons.
- 80% of learners answer the core authentication and retrieval safety scenarios correctly.
- 90% of pilot facilitators can run the workshop without maintainer intervention.
- Zero release-blocking accessibility defects in the supported browser matrix.

## Non-goals

- Providing a production customer-support backend.
- Authorizing real transactions.
- Replacing legal, privacy, security, or domain approval.

## Release gates

No public product launch until the application has browser regression coverage, accessibility review, deployment rollback, privacy-approved analytics, and a named owner for content and operations.
